# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

This is a small experimental project, and the people working on it expect
basic professional courtesy in return:

- Be direct about technical problems, kind about people.
- Critique code, storage layouts, and benchmarks - not contributors.
- Assume good faith: an odd design choice here is usually a deliberate
  trade-off, and asking why is welcome.
- Keep discussions on topic: the engine, its tests, its performance, its docs.
- No spam, no advertising, no off-topic promotion in issues or pull requests.

## Scope

The standard applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any public mention of the project.

## Reporting

If something crosses the line, open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Mudrava/esoteric-database-in-real-html-tables/issues)
or contact the repository owner directly. Reports are reviewed by the
maintainer, and the reporter's identity is not disclosed publicly.

## Consequences

The maintainer may edit or remove comments that violate this standard, and
repeat offenders may be blocked from participating. These actions are taken
to keep the project usable, not to silence disagreement.
